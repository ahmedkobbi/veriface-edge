# VeriFace Edge — Code of Conduct

## 🌟 Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone in our community — regardless of:

- Age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, or expression
- Level of experience, education, or socio-economic status
- Nationality, personal appearance, race, or religion
- Sexual identity, orientation, or any other protected characteristic

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 📜 Our Standards

### ✅ Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Be respectful** — treat everyone with respect and empathy
- **Be constructive** — give and receive feedback graciously
- **Be inclusive** — welcome newcomers and help them get started
- **Be patient** — remember that everyone has different experience levels
- **Be collaborative** — work together toward better solutions
- **Show appreciation** — acknowledge others' contributions

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of behavior that will not be tolerated:

- **Harassment** — of any form, including sexual harassment
- **Discrimination** — based on any protected characteristic
- **Personal attacks** — ad hominem arguments, name-calling
- **Trolling** — inflammatory or off-topic messages meant to provoke
- **Doxxing** — publishing others' private information
- **Spam** — unsolicited promotions, off-topic links
- **Hostile behavior** — sustained intimidation or bullying
- **Inappropriate sexual content** — in any community space

## 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions
- Code comments and commit messages
- Documentation
- Social media when representing the project
- Any other space where the community gathers

## 📋 Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## 📞 Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Private report**: Email the maintainer at `ahmedkobbi@users.noreply.github.com`
2. **GitHub report**: Use the "Report content" feature on the offending post/comment
3. **Public report** (only if safe): Open an issue tagged `conduct`

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## ⚖️ Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines when responding to violations:

### 1. Correction (First Offense)
- **Behavior**: Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior
- **Action**: Private, written warning from community leaders
- **Goal**: Clarify the nature of the violation and explain why it's unacceptable

### 2. Warning (Repeated Offense)
- **Behavior**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions
- **Action**: Warning with consequences for continued behavior
- **Goal**: Make the person understand their behavior is unacceptable

### 3. Temporary Ban (Severe or Sustained)
- **Behavior**: Severe violation or sustained inappropriate behavior
- **Action**: Temporary ban from all community spaces (1-30 days)
- **Goal**: Protect the community while the person reflects

### 4. Permanent Ban (egregious violations)
- **Behavior**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation, harassing individuals, or aggressive behavior
- **Action**: Permanent ban from all community spaces
- **Goal**: Permanently remove the threat to community safety
